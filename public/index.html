Which telecoms customers leave, and when?

A churn analysis in Python (pandas, Plotly) by Andy Alexander

Key findings

Across 7,043 customers, 26.5% churned.

Month-to-month customers churn at 42.7%, against 2.8% on Two year contracts.

Churn is highest in the first six months (52.9%) and falls to 9.5% for customers who stay beyond four years.
